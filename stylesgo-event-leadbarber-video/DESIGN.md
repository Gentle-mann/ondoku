## Style Prompt

Create a clear mobile-operations training video for a lead barber managing an Example Event in the StylesGo Barber app. The video must use real simulator captures from the StylesGo Barber Flutter app as the visual source of truth. Motion, captions, cursor clicks, and zooms can guide attention, but the app screens themselves should not be recreated or invented.

## Colors

- Background: #F2F3F4, the StylesGo app scaffold color.
- Surface: #FFFFFF for app screens and cards.
- Primary: #34A853, the StylesGo green used by the Flutter app.
- Secondary: #A8E6CF for soft success accents.
- Ink: #111827 for primary text, with #4B5563 for secondary text.
- Alert: #F59E0B for lead/live cues, #2563EB for arrival cues, #DC2626 for end/cancel warnings.

## Typography

- Use Apple system fonts: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "SF Pro Display", "Inter", sans-serif.
- Headlines are bold and compact. App labels use small, readable UI text.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use generic neon or purple-blue tech styling.
- Do not invent app labels that are not present in the captured StylesGo barber event flow.
- Do not rebuild the app UI in HTML when a captured simulator screen exists.
- Do not use decorative graphics that distract from the tap sequence.
- Do not make the video feel like a marketing landing page; this is an operational handoff.
