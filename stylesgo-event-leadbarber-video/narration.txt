Here is the lead barber flow for the Live Nation event in the StylesGo Barber app.
Open the app, tap Requests, then Events. In My events, open the Live Nation event card, and confirm attendance if the app asks.
Before start time, review venue, parking, and arrival notes. Use Chat in app or Call for anything the event manager needs to know.
When you are on site, tap I've arrived. If you are delayed, use Running late and choose an ETA so HR and StylesGo ops are notified.
When the station is set and you are ready for the first attendee, the lead barber taps Start Event.
During the event, use the participant list as the live queue. Search by name, open the three-dot menu, then Start cut, Mark done, No-show, or Reset.
At wrap-up, make sure everyone is marked correctly. The lead taps End Event. If anyone is unmarked, the app warns you before closing and recording actual time.
The simple rhythm is: arrive, start, update every participant, end clean.
